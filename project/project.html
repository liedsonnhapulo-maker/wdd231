Renewable Energy Solutions Platform

Individual Project Site Plan

Site Name

Renewable Energy Solutions Platform

The name was selected because it clearly represents a website dedicated to renewable energy technologies, including solar power systems, energy storage, and smart energy solutions. It is professional, descriptive, and easy for visitors to remember.

Site Purpose

The Renewable Energy Solutions Platform is an educational website that provides information about renewable energy technologies such as solar panels, batteries, inverters, charge controllers, and smart energy systems. Visitors will be able to explore renewable energy products, learn about their applications and benefits, and submit a contact form to request additional information or consultation.

Target Audience Scenarios

  • Which renewable energy solution is best for my home or business?
  • What are the advantages and estimated costs of installing a solar energy system?
  • Which solar products are available and what are their main specifications?
  • How can I contact the company to request a renewable energy consultation?

Color Scheme

Primary Color

#1E88E5 (Blue)

Used for the header, navigation menu, buttons, links, and page headings.

Secondary Color

#2E7D32 (Green)

Used for call-to-action buttons, icons, highlights, and important information.

Neutral Color

#F5F5F5 (Light Gray)

Used for the page background, content sections, and cards.

Typography

Poppins

Used for headings, navigation menu, buttons, and titles.

Open Sans

Used for paragraphs, forms, and general body content because it is clean and easy to read.

Website Pages

  1. Home - Introduction to renewable energy and featured renewable energy solutions.
  2. Solutions - Displays renewable energy products loaded dynamically from a JSON file with detailed information in modal dialogs.
  3. Smart Energy - Explains smart energy technologies, monitoring systems, and energy efficiency solutions.
  4. Contact - Allows visitors to request information through an online consultation form.
